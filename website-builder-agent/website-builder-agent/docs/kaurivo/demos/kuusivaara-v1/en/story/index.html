From generation to generation.

A tradition begun in 1913, carried through Turku and Itäharju to the present workshop in Littoinen.

Family business since 1913VERHOOMO KUUSIVAARA
Family history
1913

Brynolf's workshop

Brynolf Kuusivaara establishes the Fix upholstery shop in Turku and begins working with what later becomes the Korhonen furniture factory.

1930–1944

The family continues

Jorma joins the business in 1930. After the war, operations resume under the Kuusivaara name in 1944.

1985

New leadership

Pertti Kuusivaara takes over the workshop in the Itäharju wooden building.

2012

Katri and Kim

The siblings take responsibility for the family company and its upholstery traditions.

2017

A move to Littoinen

The workshop moves to the site where Artek furniture is produced.

2026

The present chapter

Katri moves away from day-to-day business, remaining involved in the background.

Original Kuusivaara archive image
Artek and Finnish design

A place in design history.

The company's history is linked to upholstery for Alvar Aalto furniture, webbing and Artek production, including work associated with the House of Culture, Finlandia Hall and Savoy Theatre.

Earlier locations

From Turku to Littoinen.

The workshop moved from central Turku to a family basement, a former chicken house and the Itäharju wooden building before relocating in 2017.

Kuusivaara.

Furniture upholstery and restoration in Littoinen, Finland. Quality since 1913.
