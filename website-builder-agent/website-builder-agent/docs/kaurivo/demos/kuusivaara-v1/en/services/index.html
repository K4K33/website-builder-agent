The process

From estimate to finished piece.

01

Contact the workshop

Describe the piece and your wishes, ideally with photographs attached.

02

Get an estimate

The work and materials are assessed individually for unusual pieces.

03

Arrange delivery

Bring the piece to Littoinen or ask about transport.

04

The upholstery work

The piece is upholstered as agreed; timing and payment arrangements are confirmed with the workshop.

Frequently asked

Before an enquiry.

Can I supply fabric?

Yes, the workshop states that customer-supplied materials are possible.

Can transport be arranged?

Ask the workshop about transport through the company or its partners.

Do you work on other brands?

Yes. The workshop upholsters furniture beyond Artek for homes and businesses.
