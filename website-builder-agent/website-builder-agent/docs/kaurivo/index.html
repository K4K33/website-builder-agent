Frequently asked questions

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Thinking about a new website or a redesign? Here are a few things businesses often ask before starting.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. We plan a suitable editing system as part of the project so you can manage everyday changes such as text, images, services and contact details without asking a developer. We can use WordPress or another appropriate CMS depending on the site.

Can you redesign our site without changing our brand?

Absolutely. If you already have a recognised logo, colours and brand identity, we can preserve them while making a substantial improvement to the layout, user experience and technical quality.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, functionality, content, integrations and design work involved. Tell us what you need and we can discuss a project-specific proposal before you commit.

How long does a project take?

The timeline depends on scope, content readiness and feedback rounds. After understanding your goals, we can agree on realistic milestones and a delivery schedule.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Mobile and desktop are both considered throughout the design and development process. We review layouts, navigation and interactions across screen sizes before handover.

What about domain, hosting and maintenance?

We will discuss suitable hosting and domain options for your project, including any ongoing third-party costs. Ownership, access and optional maintenance are agreed transparently before launch.
