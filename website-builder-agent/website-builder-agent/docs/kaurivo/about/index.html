The idea behind Kaurivo

A website
should feel
like you.

A good redesign doesn't erase everything that made your business recognisable. It takes those strengths and builds a clearer, more refined experience around them.

Before designing, we look at the business, its customers and strong examples from the same industry. We focus on what will matter to actual visitors, not what happens to look fashionable in a template gallery.

From the outset, we discuss how you can manage content yourself, which features matter most, and what happens after launch.

What to expect
