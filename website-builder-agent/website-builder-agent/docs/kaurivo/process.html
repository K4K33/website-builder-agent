How we work / 02

Thoughtful
from start to finish.

A great website is more than visual polish. Here's our straightforward approach, with room for your feedback at each important stage.

02
STEP 01 / DISCOVERY

Understand the business.

We learn about your services, your customers and what you need your website to accomplish. If you already have a site, we look at what should stay and what needs to change.

STEP 02 / DESIGN

Define the direction.

We explore layout, typography, interaction and visual hierarchy. The design should feel unmistakably yours, with clear ways for customers to act.

STEP 03 / BUILD

Build and refine.

We develop the website, refine animations, test responsive layouts and review accessibility and performance before handover.

STEP 04 / HANDOVER

Prepare for launch.

We review the result with you, agree on final changes and prepare any selected content editing platform. Hosting, ownership and support are clarified before launch.
