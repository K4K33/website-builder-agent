Ask about your project ↗
Can I edit the website myself?
Yes. We can plan a suitable content management system so your team can update text, images, services and other key information without needing to code. WordPress is one option; we'll recommend a platform based on the actual website requirements.
Can you redesign our existing website without changing the brand?
Yes. We can keep your existing logo, colour palette and visual identity where they still serve the business, while redesigning the page structure, user experience and technical implementation. A redesign should feel like your company, only better presented.
How much does a premium website cost?
The price depends on the project: number of pages, design complexity, editing system, custom functionality and integrations. Share what you need and we can discuss a project-specific proposal before any commitment. We won't quote a one-size-fits-all figure without understanding the scope.
How long does development take?
That depends on the scope, how prepared the content is and how many feedback rounds are needed. After the initial conversation, we can agree on a realistic schedule and clear milestones.
Will it work properly on mobile?
Responsive design is part of the process. We test layouts, navigation and key interactions across screen sizes. We also consider keyboard access, readable text and reduced-motion preferences.
Do the animations make websites slower?
They shouldn't. We use motion selectively and focus on efficient transform and opacity animations rather than expensive continuous effects. Every feature still needs checking on real devices and browsers, and motion can be reduced for visitors who prefer it.
What about hosting, domain and ongoing costs?
We can discuss a suitable hosting and domain setup, including any recurring costs from third parties. Website ownership, access credentials and optional maintenance are agreed as part of the project, before launch.
Can you help with future content updates?
We can discuss ongoing support, but the goal is also to give you an editing workflow for routine changes. Whether ongoing maintenance is included depends on the agreed project scope.
