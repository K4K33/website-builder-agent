Privacy,
made clear.

How project enquiries are handled through this preview site.

Who receives the information?

Kaurivo receives enquiries at hello.kaurivo@gmail.com. This preview website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

What information is submitted?

When you choose to send a project enquiry, your name, email address, company, optional existing website, type of enquiry and message are submitted through the form service. Please do not include sensitive personal details in a project enquiry.

How is it sent?

We use FormSubmit to receive contact form submissions by email. FormSubmit is a third-party provider and processes information you submit. According to the provider, it can retain submissions for up to 30 days. You may instead email us directly without using this form service.

Demo review and final-site wishes

If you review a Kaurivo demo and choose to submit your wishes for the final website, the form collects your company, reply email address, required features and any optional suggestions. The submission is handled through FormSubmit and delivered to the Kaurivo mailbox for discussion of a possible project. Alternatively, you may reply to the email that included your demo. Demo preferences are not published as testimonials, and sending a wish list does not establish a contract or authorize marketing emails.

Feedback after a confirmed purchase

We may contact an existing customer by email after a confirmed order to ask for an optional 1–5 star assessment and brief written comments about the purchasing and onboarding experience. Such replies are handled privately to improve customer service; we do not publish comments, ratings or endorsements without separate permission. The email is not a rating of an unfinished website. Customers may ignore the request and can contact Kaurivo about data use at the address above.

Private star ratings and written feedback

The Kaurivo private feedback page collects an optional project reference from the invitation link, a 1–5 star rating, written comments, company name, and a reply email. These are sent to FormSubmit for private email delivery and an external archive, subject to the form service's retention and activation rules. Kaurivo reviews and categorizes feedback for service improvement. A rating or email address is not proof of an actual purchase or the identity of the sender. Nothing is published as a testimonial without separate permission. We do not send follow-up marketing solely because a customer submitted feedback.

Why do we use it?

Project enquiry information is used to respond to your request, discuss possible work and handle related communication. We do not treat a project enquiry as permission to add you to unsolicited marketing campaigns.

Retention, access and requests

Enquiry messages may remain in the recipient mailbox while the request and any related work are handled. You can ask Kaurivo about information held or request correction or deletion, subject to legal retention needs, by emailing hello.kaurivo@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This preview does not install a Kaurivo marketing analytics script or use advertising cookies. External providers such as GitHub Pages, Google Fonts or the form service may process technical connection data under their own policies.

Preview information, last reviewed 30 October 2026. Additional formal business identification and privacy review may be required before public commercial launch.
