The details matter

Before
we begin.

Straight answers about scope, editing, costs and what happens after your site goes live.

Can I update my website myself?

Yes. We plan an appropriate editing system for your project. WordPress or another CMS may be suitable, depending on your site's needs. Everyday updates like text, images, services and contact details can be handled through the agreed editor. The exact capabilities are confirmed before development.

Can you redesign our site without changing our brand?

Yes. We can keep your established logo, colours and recognisable brand identity while substantially improving the layout, typography, navigation and mobile experience.

How much will a new website cost?

Pricing depends on the number of pages, content, design detail, integrations and other features. Share your requirements and we'll discuss a project-specific proposal before you commit to any work.

How long does a project take?

That depends on the website's scope, content readiness, feedback and technical requirements. We agree on realistic milestones and a delivery plan after reviewing your project.

Will the website work on mobile and tablets?

Responsive layout and mobile usability are part of our design and development approach. We review navigation, readability and core interactions across different screen sizes before handover.

Do you handle domains and hosting?

We can help assess suitable domain and hosting arrangements. Domain registration, hosting fees and other third-party costs are separate items and will be made clear in the proposal. Ownership and access are discussed before launch.

What about ongoing maintenance?

The site can be handed over with an agreed editing setup. If you need help with future changes, updates or maintenance, we can discuss those services separately. They aren't silently included in every project.

Will my business show up in Google?

We can plan clear page structure, semantic content and technical fundamentals that help search engines understand your website. Specific rankings and visitor growth cannot be guaranteed.

Can I contact you before I know exactly what I need?

Of course. A short description of your company, your existing website if you have one, and your main goals is a good starting point. Tell us about your project ↗

What happens to the information I submit?

We use project enquiry details to reply to your request. Our contact form uses a third-party delivery service; please read our privacy information for the details before sending.
