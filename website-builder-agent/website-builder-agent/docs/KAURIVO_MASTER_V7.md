# KAURIVO MASTER V7 — active design craft contract
Version: 7.0.5 (20 November 2026). This is a RUNTIME instruction, not merely a reference document.

MISSION: Design a credible, company-specific, commercially useful premium website. Preserve the owner's real identity and authentic strengths before adding novelty. A fully functional page is NOT necessarily well designed. The final 9.5/10 independently assessed agency benchmark is aspirational, not a numeric bar that prevents ethical early sales.

## Non-negotiable fact/rights gate
Never fabricate a service, experience duration, employee, testimonial, price, award, operating hour, map, logo, photo or case study. Do not claim a competitor's image is the company's work. An original visual or logo is usable only with evidence and reuse permissions. If rights remain unknown, record the gap and ask for company media; never silently replace with invented premises or employees. "No website found" does NOT mean "no existing brand".

## Research and media-first design
Before drawing a layout, study source screenshots, header, footer, logo, palettes, navigation, actual photographs and video, materials, texture, motion, products, conversion flow and content on the ORIGINAL site. Record a source/media passport identifying photograph subjects, source URL, usage-rights state, intended page and crop. Preserve important source strengths; a source with excellent photography or motion must not be downgraded into abstract rectangles, generic SVG art or flat text bands. When usable company photos exist, build compositions around the photos, not the reverse. When rights are unresolved, hold customer release and make an actionable asset request; an owner's noindex concept preview can have clearly distinguished provisional licensed images.

## Art direction is visual, not adjectives
Three competing directions must be VISUALLY different in composition, image strategy, type and service-page hierarchy, and rendered with real permitted source material and realistic copy for both phone and desktop. No concept wins just because it has an attractive color label. Explicitly compare first fold AND a meaningful subpage with the source and professionally reviewed references. If none is credible, reject the set rather than implementing the least bad direction. Never copy reference markup, copyrighted assets, original prose or distinctive trade dress.

## Hero composition and reference selection

Apply the [visual playbook](KAURIVO_VISUAL_PLAYBOOK.md) in concept, Build and
Revision. It defines opening compositions, colour roles, backgrounds, route
hierarchy and motion choices. Its text is part of the runtime contract digest.

Use these as composition studies for the existing three rendered concepts. Select
directions from the actual brief, available media and the visitor's first task.
Invent a more suitable direction when the source calls for it. Compare genuinely
different silhouette, focal point, type placement and content order.

| Direction | When and how to use it |
| --- | --- |
| One subject in depth | Give a real product, tool or architectural detail a clear silhouette and dominant scale. Reserve quiet space for the offer and action. Layer a backdrop and foreground only if they help frame the subject. |
| Immersive real scene | Build around a verified project, space or strong existing company video. Choose separate crops for phone and desktop. Place the offer where it stays legible throughout playback. Supply a poster and reduced-motion fallback. |
| Asymmetric visual grid | Assign clear roles to differently scaled areas: offer, dominant image, optional factual detail and action. Let the dominant image anchor the grid. On phones reorder by reading intent and remove purely decorative cells. |
| Type-led reveal | Put the company-specific offer in a distinctive typographic composition and use one relevant image or texture as a deliberate reveal. Use this when the source has strong identity or one strong close-up. Keep the offer visible without animation. |

Review a hero reference from [Supahero](https://supahero.io/) for scale, image
placement and first-fold hierarchy. Use [Land-book](https://land-book.com/) or a
live company site to study how the opening carries into services and projects.
[MotionSites](https://github.com/Motionsites-AI/claude-skill) is a source of
motion studies. Translate one purposeful behavior, such as a reveal that shows
the stages of real work. Record the observed behavior, trigger, mobile variant
and fallback. A gallery thumbnail can suggest a direction. Verify an actual
site in the browser before counting it toward existing benchmark requirements.

Choose one first action based on the visitor's task. Record its destination,
the source asset and crop, and the visual decision borrowed from each studied
reference. Review the running hero and the next section on phone and desktop:
does the offer read immediately, does the focal point survive cropping, does the
action work, and does the page continue in the same visual language? If a draft
could fit another company after a logo swap, revise the composition or media.
Review animations for smoothness and reduced-motion behavior. Preserve the
customer's logo, colors, real assets and factual content. Use original
implementation and approved media.

## Default anti-generic design decisions
Use restrained, intentional composition. A decorative 01/02/03 counter, giant "20+" visual, rotating wheel, technical gear/moon SVG, fake map, arbitrary concentric circles, repetitive dark cards, broad empty title bands, duplicate hero images, random icon gradients or a generic "Less X, More Y" slogan is a WARNING sign, not sophistication. Reject them unless a documented business, source or usability requirement justifies them. Do not reproduce an art-gallery/college aesthetic for an ordinary repair shop merely to appear expensive. Every visual element must help visitors understand an offering, trust verified real work, or take a meaningful action.

## Typography is a primary craft tool
Select families and weights consciously for the sector and brand; verify actual font files/rights, fallback behavior, rendered letter shapes, heading line breaks, optical spacing and mobile. Avoid artificially condensed/stretch-transformed lettering, extreme negative tracking, oversized headline blocks, tiny navigation, overlapping columns or paragraphs that wrap to one word per line. On 390, 768 and 1440px inspect actual text bounding rectangles, not only document scrollWidth. Headlines cannot dominate the visitor's real service answers.

## Authentic photography and motion
Use source/approved photographs before optional licensed stock. An image's filename or loading status does not make it thematically correct. Avoid invented generated staff, buildings, portfolios and company badges. Full-site design should vary appropriately between meaningful routes. Motion and 3D earn no points for existing: retain purposeful real source motion, supply reduced-motion and mobile fallback, and remove effects that look cheap, obscure content or harm responsiveness. Do not add arbitrary "premium depth" when restrained photography would be better.

### Match the interaction to the subject

- Construction and renovation: keep the page narrative anchored to a named,
  verified project. When genuine sequential photos exist, use the existing
  source-photo sequence to show the work progressing. Pair the opening with a
  project or service route that answers what was done and how to inquire.
- Product and craft: use approved imagery to show materials, parts, scale or
  the result of a process. A still composition, gallery or owned product video
  can supply depth. Reserve interactive 3D for a real model with a useful
  inspection task. Keep specifications and buying actions available without it.
- Spaces and galleries: retain useful browsing through actual rooms or projects.
  Use direct item navigation and legible labels. A drag gesture needs a
  tap/click or keyboard equivalent. Touch exploration must preserve normal
  page scrolling and offer a stable reduced-motion view.

On the rendered site, verify the interaction at the point where it is used,
including one normal-motion and one reduced-motion run. Check navigation and
the main action after the interaction. A still screenshot is evidence of
composition. A normal-motion browser run is needed to judge the transition.

## Full visual review separate from technical QA
Check at least homepage + principal service + contact/product, then every route. Review real 390/768/1440 screenshots; external hosted version matters. Evaluate enterprise identity, image authenticity, overall composition, typography, natural customer-focused Finnish/Swedish copy, navigation and CTA, useful motion, details (tab title/favicons/header/footer), and whether the site feels interchangeable/AI-generated. Record concrete observations and screenshot evidence. Give one overall quality score when legitimately reviewed, with a SEPARATE 0–10 AI-looking impression index (10 = highly formulaic looking). Do not claim an AI author detector or independent review without evidence. Pass/fail functional gates are NEVER an aesthetic score.

## Bounded improvement and release
Severe defects (false facts, image rights, 70px desktop description, fake map represented as real, broken booking, illegible UI, missing source content) block client presentation regardless of score. Generate a specific repair action from each observed defect, fix cause not the symptom, rerender and rereview changed pages; at most bounded retries, no infinite loops, no silently weakened checks. The automatic Rakenna demo owner delivery should include two verified links: original and a NEW HTTPS live preview opening as an ordinary website, with correct company title and favicon. Owner preview is not customer-approved outreach; separate owner+fact/rights approval and CMS/customer acceptance remain required.

## International references
Use the curated international benchmark library for candidate DISCOVERY and design education only; URLs with candidate status are NOT authenticated screenshot evidence or 10/10 scores. A reference may teach craftsmanship, information structure or booking flow; do not paste its brand aesthetic into unrelated Finnish SMEs. An excellent visual benchmark and a good real-user service benchmark can be different sites. Existing current-site photography and company identity always take precedence.

## Three awarded global craft calibrators — mandatory distinction
Final V7 visual QA compares the exact GENERATED site to three awarded global
craft experiences: https://landonorris.com/ (kinetic storytelling),
https://brand.dropbox.com/ (coherent brand/typography/motion), and
https://www.igloo.inc/ (purposeful visual interaction). These three sites are
a cross-industry EXECUTION-QUALITY baseline, **not** same-sector Research
references, templates, target layouts or a license to copy assets.

**Do not compare only screenshots.** Observe each real browser experience by
deliberately scrolling its entire page (including transformed/scroll-jacked
experiences), capturing desktop AND mobile viewport progress and actual browser
video; check hero-to-subpage pacing, scroll-triggered changes, navigation
feedback, image reveal timing, sticky elements, perceived smoothness,
reduced-motion alternative, coherence and use-case relevance. Save the
observations for the site's FINAL QA and compare to the generated site's
equivalent browser-scrolled journey. A collage of five captured views is
helpful evidence, but NEVER a substitute for the browser video or actual
review of the interactive experience. A video FILE existing is not proof
that a qualified reviewer watched or judged it.

Judge the Finnish client's site on equivalent *design discipline and execution
care* relative to its business, not equal visual complexity, identical scroll
physics or the quantity of animations. No fake 9.5 score, self-certified award
standard or "experience reviewed" status when reference sites cannot be opened,
video is missing, or the actual experience has not been inspected. State the
observable gap and specific business-appropriate repair actions. The automated
visual QA is separate from a genuinely independent human/external acceptance
and from authorization to sell or publish.
