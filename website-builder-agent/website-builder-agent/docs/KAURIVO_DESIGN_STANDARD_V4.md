# Kaurivo Premium Design Standard V4 — brand-led and genuinely bespoke

## Final visual pass: polish without redoing a good design

After the owner accepts a working site, keep improvements proportional. Standardize responsive image sizing and attentive art direction first, then refine typography and short transform-only hover cues if they measurably improve legibility. Preserve native scrolling, touch/reduced-motion fallback and coherent brand/copy. Do not inject new animation libraries or WebGL just to earn a model's extra half point. Verify images are not mislabeled as actual staff or past projects. A successful Kotifixi final pass is a reusable *process*, not an instruction to copy its specific blue palette onto every next client.

**Applies to ALL clients: new premium pitches, revisions, previews, developer handoffs and QA.**
**Mandatory per-client checklist:** `docs/KAURIVO_GLOBAL_DESIGN_QA_CHECKLIST.md`.
**Feedback-to-standards workflow:** `docs/KAURIVO_FEEDBACK_TO_STANDARD_WORKFLOW.md`.
Every real preview must receive a recorded PASS / FIX / N/A review; no score alone establishes production readiness.
Derived from the 2026-11-01 Kotifixi owner-feedback and observed iPhone screen recording.
This is a written acceptance standard, not evidence that every current auto-generated
website already meets it.

## Universal text discipline and proven consistent 9.5 quality

See `docs/KAURIVO_QUALITY_95_GATE.md`. Across the WHOLE site (not
per page), aim for **zero**, with **at most one expressly justified** antithesis structure such as
"Vähemmän X. Enemmän Y.", and omit it if not excellent.
Do not chain slogans, symmetrical two-clause hero lines, repeated
"yksi...monenlaisia..." phrasing or filler micro-labels. Prefer
a straightforward statement of concrete services and actual client
benefits. Review authentic Finnish and cross-page repetition.

Visual density must feel purposeful, not sparse from removing AI
decorations: build consistent editorial images, precise typography,
coherent mobile rhythm, real customer tasks and visible main CTA.
Image disclaimers can be plain adjacent microcopy, not dark
overlay "Kuvituskuva" badges. A good score for one client or on
one mobile video never represents reliable agency-wide design.
Require independent ≥9.5 and three distinct recent client demos
to meet the same standard; record any unfinished evidence as HOLD.

## V14 feedback boundary — no score-chasing redesigns

An AI's "10/10 selling demo" tips may be useful for **presentation** or **customer onboarding** rather than the website itself. When the owner approves the existing art direction, preserve it unless there is a verifiable design, accessibility, factual or technical defect. Separate current UI quality from prospect-specific materials, signed-off CMS production, genuine lead capture, and marketing performance. Convert only grounded generalizable findings into QA.

For a real prospect, use verified original logo/name, locality, phone/mail and approved imagery, never invent those facts. Do not put sample "5-star reviews" or imaginary quotes into public demos; review sections appear only when a truthful source and permission exist. Show the site live on mobile and demonstrate honest workflows as part of a real sales presentation, but do not add fake interactive conversion theater to the page.

An accessible success/error message in a test-only form must truthfully say the interaction is **test-only**. Do not claim it reached a customer unless delivery was proven. Keep the approved existing Kotifixi V14 unchanged based on this feedback alone; owners receive the same preview URL until an actual next version is warranted.

## V14 — header controls form one deliberate system

Match action color hierarchy, button height, corner shape, inset and font weights to the website's actual typography. One grouped phone + quote control is often calmer than two differently styled floating boxes: use a shared subtle tint/border where helpful, a secondary phone action and one primary branded CTA, with two real accessible click targets and appropriate focus states. Do not universally force this exact Kotifixi design on other brands; verify on desktop, tablet and mobile.

Independent review notes about generic stock or sample notices should lead to factual photography and real client content collection **after actual interest**, never hiding preview warnings or generating fake proof. Test a real CMS, branded HTTPS link and lead contact behavior before claiming sales readiness.

## V13 — visual distribution beats mathematical centering

A perfectly centered navigation can still look awkward when its first link sits hundreds of pixels away from the logo. Select the layout by measured content bounds and user feedback, not by a rigid grid pattern. In a horizontal three-area header, try `max-content minmax(0,1fr) max-content`: let site navigation distribute its links naturally between brand and contact actions. Preserve 16–17px body/nav weights, visible focus and appropriate minimum click targets and logo safety space. Review genuine desktop and mobile screenshots at multiple widths and browser zoom levels, especially the blank region the reviewer points at, before acceptance.

Keep proven motion and native scrolling; do not add motion to solve a spacing problem. Respect source language and the owner's accepted copy even when a model prefers a rejected alternative. Do not mistake private screen-capture notifications for website UI.

## V12 — proportional navigation and matching fonts

Premium headers balance a real brand/logo area, precisely centered five-link navigation, and independently right-justified call-to-action/contact elements. A three-track CSS Grid is often stronger than a flex menu pushed away by margin-left:auto. The exact solution may differ between brands, but test geometry rather than assuming that equal gutters imply balanced middle content. Handle responsive widths by hiding secondary header phone contact, not shrinking all links or creating overlap.

One deliberate typography system: body/nav DM Sans around 600, strong but less bulky Manrope display headings around 700, intentional letter spacing and active states. Adapt to the specific client's brand rather than universally copying a font. Respect natural Finnish and the approved current heading; a third-party AI suggestion contradicting explicit owner feedback does not override it.

Keep good existing CSS depth and native scroll. Verify full desktop, mobile and animation states on the exact version. Treat browser-generated translation overlays as external UI unless page language/markup facts prove otherwise.

## V11: premium depth, motion and Finnish clarity without scroll cost

Natural language comes before slogans. Prefer a first-screen question describing verified household work, services or the visitor's next action over repeated business name or generic motivational lines. Check consistency across all pages.

Use premium but measured depth where visual storytelling calls for it: one hero layered photo/backplate, a slight CSS 3D tilt for pointer-capable desktop users, meaningful card elevation and sensitive one-time reveals. No continuous 3D scene, default heavy WebGL, scroll hijacking or unconditional animation. Transforms are local and disabled for touch/reduced-motion/no-JS as appropriate. Test real scrolling and load speed rather than claiming improvement from source changes.

Keep navigation typography intentionally large, 17–18px when spatially feasible, keyboard readable and not crammed. Route labels must match actual provided content (a genuine About page needs genuine verified people/business history; process pages should be named for process). Treat browser translation controls as browser UI; do not disable legitimate translation without consent or reason.

## V10 — independent human assessment and legibility

Independent human first-impression feedback counts alongside model reviews. Desktop navigation uses comfortably readable text, typically 15–16px, verified at tablet and desktop widths. Phone/contact controls should be presented as one deliberate set of actions, not an orphaned phone number.

Write natural, non-repetitive Finnish. Avoid mechanically repeating locative phrases and avoid third-person reporting of the client's own website. Do not invent unverified first-person company claims.

Allow tasteful one-time reveals on below-the-fold content using IntersectionObserver, opacity and transforms, while retaining native scrolling, a disabled-animation path and visible content without JavaScript. Include purposeful hover, click and keyboard focus states. Review all visible third-party marks in example photography.

Every completed reviewable version must have a published, verified internal preview URL delivered in the same response. A customer-sales URL still requires separately approved branded HTTPS hosting.

## Subsequent V9 audit — distinguish delivery quality from client-supplied materials

Assess the actual scope the agency can control: visual hierarchy, accurate business information supplied so far, navigation, device usability, accessibility, performance and frontend integration. Keep unknown customer-specific people/credentials/reviews visibly **absent**, not invented, until consent/verified assets arrive. Mark them as *customer content pending*, not a reason to insert fake text or a stock portrait.

Photo areas need stable responsive aspect ratios, cropping, light color treatment and consistent radii so authorized client images can be substituted without redesigning layouts. Omit empty optional team/review/FAQ sections until content exists.

Preview mode must state that the site is a demo and use only clearly identified test pathways. Production mode cannot be enabled by hiding banners; require signed-off content, domain, secure live form and backend, privacy/SEO, real CMS/owner editing and removal of **all** preview/test text and endpoints at build time. Do not publish the customer variant until verified.

## V9: honest premium page rhythm, native scroll and client-ready preview trust

- Design information blocks according to **reading priority**, not a repeating card recipe. Separate source notes and tax guidance from pricing actions, keep enough line length and spacing, and avoid stacks of pale-grey full-width strips.
- Trackpad/wheel/touch interaction should feel native. Do not use blanket smooth scrolling, per-scroll paint changes or backdrop-filter-heavy sticky UI merely for polish. Measure actual physical-device performance before claiming speed.
- The first viewport should show real services and coherent photos, with natural Finnish, readable small text and restrained heading treatments. An illustration must not accidentally imply an employee, owner or past client work.
- An **internal technical URL** is not a customer-appropriate preview: raw.githack warning/interstitial, github.io repository paths and developer-domain URLs cannot go in outreach. Check the actual first-open URL, direct HTTPS load and verified branded domain, then secure explicit send approval. Do not assume a domain or DNS is already purchased.
- Human context (founder name, photos, verified reviews, service areas, tax eligibility) raises credibility *only if factual*. Missing evidence must be reported as such rather than filled with stock claims. Do not add personal-data form fields without purpose and backend/privacy support.
- Preserve QA gate: client outreach cannot proceed until externally verified >=9.5/10 on latest exact build and stable cross-project quality.

## Brand integrity ≠ conservative visuals

Verify the customer's **real** logo, brand color, imagery and locked copy before
starting. Branded material from an industry competitor cannot substitute for the
customer's identity. A major redesign must substantially improve hero,
composition, content architecture, visual storytelling, mobile usability and
conversion path **while protecting** approved names, logos, photos and phrases.
A logo made from an invented star/house/wrench glyph is not a verified original.
When true brand assets are unavailable: use typography-only name as a marked
temporary proposal and obtain approval before production.

## Never use decorative numbering everywhere

- No mechanical numbering of every hero, service card, label, section,
  image or footer (01 / 02 / 03 or similar).
- Sequence numbers are allowed only when they clarify an **actual ordered
  process**, such as the three steps of ordering a service.
- Remove repeated all-caps micro-labels and gratuitous tiny captions.
  Real service information, editorial composition and clear hierarchy win.
- Human QA must reject a page that feels assembled from repeated AI-looking
  templates, even if HTML passes validation.

## Professional information architecture and navigation

For a multi-page customer scope, use genuine independent page URLs with a
persistent, accessible navigation bar (e.g. /palvelut/, /meista/, /yhteys/).
Do not simulate separate pages by linking a menu to anchors on one
extremely long landing page. Home can still use reasonable vertical scroll;
it should not swallow the entire site's structure.

**Important current limitation:** `agents/builder.py` still only generates
one self-contained `index.html` file; its QA and saved-output format expect
one page. **It cannot be considered a production-ready multi-page builder.**
Until a separate multi-page build/QA output contract is implemented, create
multi-page demonstrations through a separate reviewed workflow and do not claim
automatic agent parity. Never alter output format by prompt-only edits that
would break QA, preview or the existing stable pipeline.

## Cross-industry, human-first visual clarity and conversion gate

- In the **first mobile viewport**, show what the business does, who it serves
  and the next useful action in plain words. Brand slogans alone cannot
  replace the offer. Reveal actual verified services early.
- Prevent *generic premium aesthetic*: symbolic fake logos, luxury lifestyle
  images unrelated to the work, repeated micro-labels and filler headings
  undermine authenticity. Customer-owned authentic visual materials take
  priority when rights and approval are verified.
- Give one coherent, clearly named primary action per context. Make contact
  and booking paths genuinely usable; test submissions if advertised.
- Review **every link destination** in context, especially external pricing
  pages, stale legacy-site URLs, contact endpoints and phone numbers.
  A legacy link is acceptable only if correctly labeled and intentional.
- Verify actual first-load/cold-start behavior on realistic hosting; a free
  preview's slow startup is a demo problem, not an acceptable production
  performance promise. Benchmarking must use real measurements and declare
  device/network.
- Independent AI review is evidence only when a distinct real reviewer/model
  has actually reviewed the supplied artifact. Do not fabricate opinions,
  prices or analysis. Human brand/customer approval remains necessary.

## Sitewide media and wayfinding QA

- Review the **whole website**, not each page in isolation. Repeating the
  same editorial photograph in multiple places creates a template impression.
  Deduplicate by normalized asset URL/source image ID, including query
  string variants; preserve repeated verified company logo/icons as identity.
- Replace unnecessary "Tutustu" / "Learn more" links with specific user
  tasks such as "Palvelut", "Hinnasto", "Soita" or "Kysy arviota".
  Every CTA must advance the visitor, not just lead to another generic panel.
- Check the shortest natural route: offer and services → pricing if useful →
  direct contact. No maze of redundant subpages, mystery buttons or
  repeated destination links.

## V7 premium visual richness without synthetic AI aesthetics

The goal is deliberately high visual quality **and** a human, company-specific
appearance. Less AI-looking does **not** mean bare white cards, removed
typographic hierarchy, or weak business CTAs. Build a consistent art direction
with strong authentic brand colors, a recognizable silhouette and composition,
considered whitespace, functional animation, responsive contrast and real
service evidence. Where company pictures are unavailable, ask only interested
clients for approved photos using
`docs/KAURIVO_CLIENT_PHOTOGRAPHY_INTAKE.md` or design with fewer,
coherently sourced images. Do not mix unrelated stock imagery.

Remove *decorative* arrows without removing the actual visual affordances
of links and buttons. The important first action must remain prominent.
An external mobile-video-only AI review is not proof of desktop visual
performance, photo rights, real CMS, brand approval or conversion outcomes.

## Motion and visual polish

- Design a deliberate motion choreography: opening entrance, section
  disclosure, hover/focus feedback, optional image pan, responsive navigation.
- Use transforms and opacity, avoid layout shift, unnecessary continuous
  looping, janky scroll hijacking or expensive WebGL by default.
- Preserve functionality if JavaScript fails. Honour
  `prefers-reduced-motion`, show motion alternatives and keyboard focus.
- Check images load, crop and composition work for 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px.
  Unlicensed or unrelated stock photography must be marked as a proposal.
- The brand determines palette and art direction; do not force green or
  Kaurivo's own identity on client brands.

## Human visual acceptance gate

Open the **actual browser-rendered** preview (not PDF/screenshot alone) and
check menu, links, CTAs, imagery, logo fidelity, touch targets, layout overflow,
reduced-motion, form truthfulness and mobile readability. Compare with three
independent real same-industry references. Document actual site shortcomings
and what the proposal improves. No fake testimonials, awards, lead metrics or
imagined features.

An unlisted URL with `noindex` is still **public to anyone who has the URL**.
Never call it a private secure preview. Request owner authorization before
sharing unpublished branded material, use real access controls for genuinely
private client files. No production domain changes without explicit approval.
