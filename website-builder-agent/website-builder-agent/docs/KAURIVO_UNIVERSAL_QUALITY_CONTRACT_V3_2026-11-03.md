# Kaurivo — universal quality contract V3 (3 November 2026)

**Scope:** Every new and redesigned company site, every sector, every page, every delivery route. This is not a reusable design template, a promise of automatic 10/10 design, or a client-facing text block.

## How quality is evaluated

Never substitute a high average for a material failure. Results have **three separate statuses**:

1. **BLOCKED**: fabricated or contradictory facts, missing verified original content, false brand, lost partner/logo/price/review evidence, misleading stars or location, broken primary conversion, unlawful or unauthorized publication, critical accessibility problems or invented reference work. Stop the publish path, correct the underlying source/build fault, add a cross-sector regression.
2. **REVIEW REQUIRED**: sourced details or permissions not yet independently verified, semantic rewrites, page-specific design quality, visual balance, real form delivery, CMS edits, browser-rendered widgets or performance/rights evidence unavailable. A demo may be shared *only with a clear separate preview disclosure and without verified-grade claims*.
3. **CLIENT-READY**: zero blockers, all source and design evidence completed, real interactions tested, owner authorization tied to the current **full build and original-source digest**. An unrelated synthetic test case cannot substitute for a real company review.

**No weighted-averaging away hard fails.** An impressive animation cannot compensate for a missing sponsor, inaccessible text, false review, incomplete price list, bad booking link or generic AI-generated substitute for a brand.

## Exact release protocol

**Before generation:** verified business facts → multi-page source crawl → content/brand/review/footer manifests → three real same-sector benchmarks → three significantly different concept plans → source-linked sitemap and section wireframes. If an authoritative old page cannot be read, carry it as an explicit research gap; do not replace it with model imagination.

**During generation:** use verified facts only; make every substantive original content block discoverable at its new route; avoid sitewide repeated generic messages and identical section composition. The source map and verified brand kit are constraints, not content to show in the visitor-facing copy.

**Before a first demo:** run deterministic multi-page checks, locate broken/unauthorized media and unsupported social proof, compare key content units, inspect page/screenshots at mobile/tablet/desktop and check the core user journeys. A demo with pending client rights or subjective review can be offered as an *unapproved concept*, never as a customer-ready production website.

**Before production:** verify consent, original assets, all content rewrites, customer trust/rating provenance, exact event/booking/contact destinations, real form delivery, CMS edit-and-rollback, domain, privacy and independent human/AI design reviews. Customer acceptance must match the current full build and current original research snapshot. Any content or layout change invalidates that acceptance until re-reviewed.

## Independent 9.5/10 target

A numerical claim requires two distinct reviewer identities, traceable screenshot evidence at four widths **for every route**, all ten existing premium dimensions at **≥9.5 independently for each reviewer**, zero critical issues and exact source/build verification. Neither a green CI suite nor a score fabricated by the builder fulfills this.

To call the process **reliably** 9.5/10 across industries, demonstrate real first-pass performance on at least three *unrelated*, never-previously-iterated company cases and report misses as well as successes. Do not rework only the same example until it passes; make each critique a cross-industry rule with positive, negative and false-positive-control regression cases.

## Newly automated in V3

- **Invented testimonials:** recognized review cards are compared against the original source review inventory. A new quote requires a source URL, rights verification and a client review signature tied to current page digest and original snapshot.
- **Inflated stars:** a previously unrated original quote cannot gain 5 stars; a four-star original cannot become a five-star testimonial. The value is checked *at the quote's own card*.
- **Unverified Google-like rating badges:** aggregate score summaries require independent source verification and client signoff. Decorative star icons or generic literary quotations are not automatically called customer ratings.
- **Dynamic widgets:** uninspectable testimonial widgets remain explicitly pending a rendered/browser verification, never counted as proof of no reviews.
- **CI contract:** modify any social-proof guard or its tests → run dedicated regressions, full quality workflow also runs independently.

These deterministic checks prevent recognizable failure classes. They cannot prove every customer's authorship, licence, visual beauty or dynamic third-party rating feed on their own.
