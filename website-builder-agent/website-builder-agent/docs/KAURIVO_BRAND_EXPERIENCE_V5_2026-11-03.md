# Kaurivo V5 — brand experience, first mobile view and contextual voice

Universal standard, 2026-11-03. Applies to every company redesign, regardless of industry.

## Why this rule exists

An unsolicited florist demo looked recognisably floral, preserved the
observed source emblem and all 45 source gallery images, and passed extensive
programmatic/browser QA. Yet critical comparison of a supplied mobile screen
recording showed a substantial change in **brand character**: the original
soft dusty-pink header and handwritten Karjalanneito wordmark became a bold
all-caps dark plum identity. The new phone first view prioritised a large
dark text region and CTAs, delaying authentic source flower imagery below
the first viewport. Several lines sounded like replaceable marketing filler.
This is an editorial / visual QA failure that green code tests cannot detect.

The corrective rule is GENERAL: a logo file preserved in an asset registry
is not proof that the original customer's brand experience was preserved.

## Brand DNA, not logo-file matching

Before the builder runs, capture with evidence:

1. **Full brand lockup and hierarchy**: full wordmark, original letterforms
   or type treatment, emblem, relative proportions, spacing, contrast,
   original treatments in header/footer, and whether the original mark is
   photographic, illustrated or typographic. A preserved tiny logo symbol
   plus invented bold wordmark may still be an unauthorised rebrand.
2. **Palette as a relationship**: primary background, secondary surfaces,
   accent, contrast and visual softness/energy. An observed muted pale-pink
   brand does NOT automatically authorise a dark burgundy identity just
   because both are shades of red or pink.
3. **Original photographic signature**: hero imagery, storefront/workspace,
   product photography, source crop and subject prominence. Genuine source
   photos are not interchangeable decorations.
4. **Customer voice**: exact naturally spoken descriptions, heritage and
   locality, whether brand tone is playful / intimate / technical / modest
   / exuberant. Do not replace all original text with the agency's slogan style.
5. **Emotional context**: the same company may serve different journeys.
   Funeral/sympathy pages require sensitive, restrained copy and clear
   compassionate contact. A wedding service can use a livelier treatment.
   Do not show a random playful sales composition for every need.
6. **Business tasks**: real first actions, pricing or question path,
   visit/address/hours, stock/order constraints. Never invent delivery
   promises, product inventory, prices, staff, guarantees or checkout.
7. **Rights**: the source URL is not a copyright licence. Hotlinked source
   photographs/wordmarks on publicly accessible demos remain rights pending
   until lawful use/explicit permission is established. Noindex is not
   permission. Client-ready requires an actual client decision.

## Five-second phone test

For visually demonstrative sectors (florists, salons, food, craft,
retail), inspect a **real 320/390px screenshot** before and after opening
the new page, including the first practical viewport. Does the user see
the customer's actual work or product with sufficient prominence? If
the above-the-fold content is only large lettering, dark bars and CTA
buttons, escalate for critical human review. Do not mechanically forbid
text-led first views for legal, emergency or text-service businesses.

Check the original full-wordmark treatment *side by side*, not merely whether
an original emblem image URL is present.

Measure the proportion of first-view screen space spent on legal
disclosures, headers, ornamental banners, explanatory marketing and
real business visuals. Disclosures must be legible and truthful but
not consume the entire business first impression. Never hide a legally
necessary disclosure.

## Voice acceptance

Reject or explicitly rewrite context-free lines that could be transplanted
unchanged between a florist, university and contractor. Examples:
- 'Karhulassa, oikeista kukista': artificial or tautological without
  business purpose.
- 'Teemme hetkestä oman': generic emotion without identifying service.

Prefer genuine client phrasing and service specificity. Every prominent
headline should answer a visitor question or express documented client
personality. Do not invent quotes or use creative invented copy as if
it were original brand copy.

## Composition acceptance

A recurring recipe (dark left-text hero, bright CTA, thin info ticker,
large paper-colour service list, full-width editorial panel, mosaic,
final dark CTA) is an agency house-template even with genuine photos.
The research must explain why each major section format fits the
client; visually compare the new site with at least two unrelated
prior Kaurivo clients and their original sites. Challenge repeated
section rhythms, card proportions, heading typography and always-on
pill buttons.

No automatic ban on necessary price lists, galleries, cards or navigation
patterns: usefulness and business evidence trump arbitrary uniqueness.

## Verification and release

- **BLOCKED:** invented brand mark, altered source identity without
  client approval, fabricated facts or copyrighted assets used without
  lawful permission in a release.
- **REVIEW REQUIRED:** full-wordmark typography and palette fidelity,
  sector appropriate first-fold media, authentic voice, sensitive
  service journeys, visual similarity to unrelated Kaurivo sites,
  media licence, two independent screenshot reviewer observations.
- **ELIGIBLE FOR CLIENT REVIEW only after evidence:** screenshots at
  320, 390, 768, 1440; source and redesigned views side by side; all
  major source logos/assets/copy; screenshot-based brand personality
  checks; owner approval where identity changed; page purpose/CTA
  clarity and image captions; exact-build QA linked to source hash.

Automated test passes are not equivalent to 9.5/10 artwork.
Scores from a reviewer inspecting a short recording are preliminary
and cannot certify the desktop implementation or all legal rights.
