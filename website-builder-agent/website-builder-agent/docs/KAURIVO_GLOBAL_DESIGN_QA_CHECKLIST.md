## V3: source-bound trust claims and non-compensatory quality gate

The current universal acceptance contract is
**docs/KAURIVO_UNIVERSAL_QUALITY_CONTRACT_V3_2026-11-03.md**.
It applies to **every industry and every route**, including future first-pass demos.
A high overall score never compensates for fake ratings, missing original
information, an invented logo, unlawful imagery, broken contact/booking flow,
an unverified sponsor, bad readability or absent client approval.

- [ ] Every original testimonial that is reused retains the actual **associated** source stars (including an unrated original remaining unrated).
- [ ] Every **new** testimonial has a real evidence URL, reuse rights and current source/build-bound client approval; no synthetic customer quotes.
- [ ] Every numerical Google-like/aggregate rating badge has verified source evidence and an approval for the exact current build.
- [ ] Empty or JS-only review widgets are browser/human **REVIEW REQUIRED**, never automatically certified.
- [ ] Source, rights, interaction and accessibility blockers are resolved before any customer-ready claim; do not use a 9.5/10 average to hide them.
- [ ] All old-to-new source content, affiliates and logos are audited on subpages as seriously as on the homepage.
- [ ] 320/390/768/1440 screenshots cover every route; two independent reviewer reports are tied to the exact build if claiming >=9.5 in each of ten dimensions.
- [ ] All QA regressions apply across companies, with source-linked positive, negative and false-positive controls.

## Premium 9.5/10 first-pass standard — 2026-11-03

This is a source-first workflow for ALL future clients, not a patch to an old
demo. Technical CI, genuine visual quality, source completeness and legitimate
customer acceptance are different evidence gates.

1. Crawl original homepage and important subpages; record and report unread
   URLs, JS-loaded content and any 30k text / source-block caps. Keep sections,
   original product and service categories, prices, caveats, owners, staff, logo,
   colors, actual photos, affiliation marks, testimonials, exact rating stars,
   booking and contact links, FAQs and substantial paragraphs.
2. Produce the old-to-new matrix: for every source page and source heading
   specify a real new destination; every nontrivial paragraph/list item is
   retained verbatim or mapped to a source/build-specific human-approved rewrite.
   Never certify paraphrase equivalence by a word-count or similarity heuristic.
3. The Brand DNA brief identifies original visual assets, typography, colors,
   actual photography and tone. Unlicensed photos require permission or a
   legitimate owner-approved replacement, not an invented stock employee.
4. Explore three genuinely different site architectures and justify the selected
   original concept using sector benchmarks and first-party customer evidence.
5. Use real quotes + their original explicit star counts only; never invent
   social proof, five-star ratings or staff identities.
6. Editorial proof: eliminate template slogans, repeated arrows, needless card
   numbering, redundant filler, unexplained changes to original phrases and
   content omissions. Preserve all verified accurate facts.
7. Verify a complete primary visitor task inventory with the exact company
   booking URL, phone, email, checkout or contact path; inspect actual keyboard,
   small-mobile and desktop behavior, not just clickable-looking buttons.
8. Run automated and actual browser checks; hold unknown contrast, media rights,
   navigation, focus, real lead delivery and CMS access for human verification.
9. Performance: measure lab results before launch; NEVER treat them as real-user
   CWV field data. At adequate field sample size, independently verify p75
   LCP <= 2500ms, INP <= 200ms and CLS <= 0.1 against that exact site build.
10. For new premium owner approval, require the complete source/build-specific
    human coverage review AND two independent, provenance-checked reviewers.
    Both score EACH of ten dimensions >= 9.5/10, provide exact-build screenshots
    at desktop, tablet, mobile and 320px, and inspect all pages. Averages cannot
    mask weak dimensions. Reviewers must not be the builder, and ratings cannot
    be fabricated by test fixtures or code.
11. No-site companies still need verifiable factual source evidence and a
    build-specific human factual review before acceptance.
12. Any unresolved visual defect, missing factual source, or critical usability
    bug blocks final acceptance even if the full Python/Playwright suite is green.
    The client must be able to change real text/images/contact info using a
    tested CMS account, with backups, rollback and a handover guide.

**Release distinctions:** A fresh demo can exist with clearly pending visual,
legal, rights, field-metric and human checks. This MUST NOT be described as a
verified 9.5/10 client-ready site. New premium approvals are fail-closed.


## Company-first brand and content preservation
Before any redesign, keep the original company identity, source-page inventory,
logo candidates, colors, real staff images, complete services, original prices
and verified customer feedback. Never replace real company identity with
generic stock art or initials. Where rights or visual equivalence are uncertain,
leave customer release pending instead of inventing content.

Every sourced testimonial must retain its real text and any actual displayed
star score. A real 5-star review keeps five meaningful, accessible stars.
A review without a verified score must not receive an invented star rating.

The Builder requires an original-brand plan tied to actual source colors,
logos, and URLs. Whole-site automated checks compare original reviews,
rating evidence, explicit pricing table rows, and FAQ question labels.
Exact-source checks supplement but do not replace human semantic review.

- [ ] Compare source and redesigned product/service tables row-by-row: full verified prices, units, size bands, weight limits, breed/product names, dates, eligibility and exceptions.
- [ ] Creative shortening must not invent a different real-world category. The salon's published labradoodle mini/medium was incorrectly changed to lagotto mini / medium and caught in expert review; regression test now blocks recurrence in that demo.
- [ ] Put high-risk source labels and price examples into evidence-bound content_requirements for every new build, and keep separate human source-inventory signoff. A green generic unit suite cannot substitute for business-specific source accuracy.


## Decorative-arrow repetition: agency-wide blocking rule (2026-11-03)
- [ ] No generic ↗/→/➜ arrow appended to every CTA, service row, contact link or footer link. Repeated arrow-led button design is a **failed visual QA**, even if all functional tests pass.
- [ ] The shared `non_repetitive_cta_icons` code gate rejects more than two arrow-labelled links across any rendered site, or any repeated same arrow link. A functional single directional navigation control can be justified; screenshots still receive independent review.
- [ ] Review pseudo-element `content: '↗'`, CSS icon masks, SVG arrows and hover states: HTML glyph counting alone cannot establish clean visual design.
- [ ] For every user report, add the generalized code check, a unit regression and a specific demo regression. A green build without the new regression test is insufficient.

## Independent mobile review learning (2026-11-02) — universal blockers

- [ ] **Finish Finnish headings manually:** no mid-syllable / mid-word forced breaks in prominent headings. Test real wrapping at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px; CSS `hyphens: auto` / `overflow-wrap: anywhere` is not a universal fix. Prefer shorter headings, natural breaks and `text-wrap: balance` where supported.
- [ ] **Use italics sparingly:** avoid repeated cursive/serif italic fragments in every section; retain only genuine editorial emphasis (normally at most two primary heading accents on a single page) and verify readability on phones.
- [ ] **Vary authentic sentence structure:** avoid repeated company-name openers, repeated third-person distance and formulaic section rhythm. Write natural Finnish appropriate for the company's verified voice. Do not impersonate an unapproved client or fabricate commitments.
- [ ] **Image-subject plausibility:** a picture must reflect the likely scale of the actual service and audience, not merely loosely match a keyword (e.g. giant commercial mower for small household lawn service). Prefer a better-fit licensed example or remove the misleading image; never pretend stock is client work.
- [ ] **Proof over praise:** add genuine verified client reviews or identifiable case-study evidence only when available and permissioned. If unavailable, omit; no fictional 5-star reviews, numbers or anonymous testimonials to raise an AI score.
- [ ] **Legal/financial customer benefits:** verify every tax percentage and threshold from a dated primary authority, distinguish eligible work from excluded work and verify seller registration before claiming eligibility. Source-linked neutral guidance may be used with an explicit eligibility caveat; never copy stale figures from other AI reviews.
- [ ] **Reviewer scope and version:** video review of V7 is NOT a score for V8. Compare criticism with latest exact source before editing and record fixes versus already-addressed issues.

## 2026-11-02 — AI feedback is a hypothesis, not a redesign mandate

- [ ] **Evaluate the actual category of the score:** distinguish design quality, sales-demo usefulness, live production readiness and client-specific personalization. An overall sales-demo score (e.g. 8.5/10) is not a verified design-performance score or proof of revenue conversion.
- [ ] **Owner acceptance matters:** when owner and credible user feedback say a working version is good, DO NOT redesign just to get an AI's 10/10. Record supported defects, fix genuine conversion/accessibility/technical failures, and avoid unrequested elements. The independently evaluated >=9.5 threshold remains a separate formal release goal; never fabricate a score or ignore an actual blocker.
- [ ] **Client identity is conditional:** when building a new company's demo, use ONLY verified company name, logo rights, email/phone, geography and service claims. Don't replace the accurately identified subject business with an unrelated prospect's brand. Missing verified locality is a factual research question, not permission to invent place-specific promises.
- [ ] **Never fake social proof:** no example testimonials written as though real customers said them, even under the pretext "they can replace the examples later". If an empty review module is truly necessary for a separately approved prototype, use neutral structural scaffolding in internal design tooling, never quote-like copy in the public demo. Omit unused review modules in sales/public previews.
- [ ] **Sell through genuine live interaction:** in human-reviewed sales meetings, demonstrate actual mobile layout, tested navigation and real action destinations, stating clearly what is demonstration-only and what is production-ready. No fabricated "customer clicked" claims and no simulated orders disguised as leads.
- [ ] **Form-feedback truth:** if the page includes a contact form, confirm accessible success, validation and error states in a safe mock/dry-run before any real integrations; success message accurately says test capture rather than falsely promising delivery to the business. Live form routing requires explicit approval and end-to-end verification.
- [ ] **Quality gate must not be gamed:** no inflated AI scores or fake review/testimonial data, no suppressing truthful demo labels, and no fabricated promises of 100% conversion. Collect missing assets only after genuine interest/authorization.

## 2026-11-02 V14 — coherent contact-action styling and useful external review

- [ ] **Integrated header actions:** the phone and quote/contact CTA must visually belong to the same intentional system. When both appear, one lightly outlined group with consistent button heights, corner radii, typographic weights and clear space can outperform separate default-looking boxes. Each action remains independently accessible and clickable.
- [ ] **Do not create false dual CTAs:** only the primary conversion action uses strong brand color; the telephone action stays quieter but still visibly interactive. Never sacrifice contrast/focus, hide the caller behind a hover-only affordance or make both buttons look like competing primaries.
- [ ] **Responsive fallback:** on smaller desktops, the top bar may show only the main action, with the telephone still reachable in mobile navigation and elsewhere. At 320–430px the menu toggle must remain separate, functional and a comfortable target. Check that the action group does not overlap the logo or navigation.
- [ ] **Owner's actual visual feedback overrides template taste:** an AI can compliment a technically correct site and still miss mismatched buttons. Review phone/button grouping in genuine desktop and mobile screenshots; reject a fix if the controls are merely nested in another decorative card.
- [ ] **External AI observations, not automatic changes:** demo disclaimers and Kaurivo labels are legally important honest disclosures in owner previews, not "AI mistakes" to delete. Generic stock is acceptable only when truthful, rights-cleared and locally plausible; seek original client media after genuine interest. Do not fabricate staff, testimonials, service promises or turnaround metrics to look human.
- [ ] **Sales-readiness separate from design polish:** free internal review links, test contact backend, website design score and a hypothetical offer do not prove a real customer-facing CMS, own domain, live lead routing or client-approved pricing. Verify all before handover/outreach.

## 2026-11-02 V13 — use the real available header width, not false geometric symmetry

- [ ] **Eliminate dead pointer zones:** inspect the actual first-link gap after the brand and the last-link gap before contact actions. Equal-width outside grid columns can mathematically center navigation while leaving a 200–300px void by the logo. Prefer content-sized brand/action tracks with a flexible distributed navigation track when it better balances the visible composition.
- [ ] **Measured criteria:** check the gap from logo right edge to first link and from last link to contact action at the actual desktop rendered size. In a five-link evenly distributed menu these should normally be under about 50 CSS pixels (adjust for specific client art direction), with nonoverlapping hit areas, sufficient whitespace and matching page gutters. Do not equate CSS `justify-content:center` with visual balance.
- [ ] **Responsive breadth:** verify widths 320/360/390/430/768/940/960/1024/1180/1280/1440/1920, horizontal overflow, the 940px mobile-menu breakpoint, keyboard/tab order, 44px touch areas where appropriate, and zoom/font scaling. Browser tests support a visual review, not replace it.
- [ ] **Reviewer disagreement:** do not change a user-approved headline because a model prefers a previously rejected locative phrase. Distinguish a screen recording's browser WhatsApp overlay and capture blur from actual site defects. Model price estimates are not approved service quotes.
- [ ] **Independent feedback scope:** if an external reviewer did not inspect the latest mobile build, record that explicitly and do not certify mobile or 9.5/10 based on desktop or a model's "ready to sell" claim.
- [ ] **Every version's working link:** give the exact versioned owner preview proactively and verify published source and first-load behavior; internal GitHub Pages addresses are never links for sales prospects.

## 2026-11-02 V12 review — symmetrical header and compatible type system

- [ ] **Horizontal balance:** use a centered navigation region with independent logo and action regions on wide screens, not a growing logo-to-menu gap caused by `margin-left:auto`. At 1024, 1180, 1280 and 1440 px measure actual rendered menu midpoint, element bounds and clipping. Do not rely on a subjective "looks centered" note alone.
- [ ] **Typography belongs to one coherent system:** select one compatible sans family for navigation/body and a deliberate heading face; avoid mismatched 800-weight condensed-looking menu letters beside a lighter button. Check weight, x-height, tracking, active state and line height on rendered devices. Font uniformity does not mean every element has identical weight.
- [ ] **Header CTA grouping:** keep the contact button group right aligned with proper right gutter, while independently centering navigation. Phone and quote link must share visual language; at narrow desktop widths collapse the phone into the responsive menu if required rather than shrinking the nav.
- [ ] **Brand presence without invented assets:** keep authorized logo/palette and refine subtle blue framing, focus, underline and alignment rather than adding fake symbols. Use eye-catching yet restrained accents only if they support actual brand.
- [ ] **Reported browser translation bar:** `<html lang="fi">` and UTF-8 already define the website language; browser translation prompts may still be caused by browser state or bilingual browser preferences. Inspect actual page source before blaming the site, never attempt to suppress user translation UI, and avoid unsupported diagnosis.
- [ ] **Avoid unrequested product scope:** suggestions to stop iteration or set package pricing are not verified technical fixes or authorized customer offers. Independent reviewers assess actual version and scope; measured performance and current mobile QA remain independent blockers.

## 2026-11-02 V11 review — question-first service copy, deeper interaction, no scroll jank

- [ ] **Hero is specific and human:** state verifiable service categories in natural Finnish, ideally as a customer question or another direct task-focused title. Avoid a vague self-referential company-name slogan, but keep approved brand language unchanged. Watch repetition sitewide rather than swapping between two equally awkward synonyms.
- [ ] **Desktop legibility:** re-evaluate header after every revision: where it fits cleanly aim for 17–18px top-level navigation. At narrower widths select smaller readable labels or the mobile menu; never squeeze navigation with tiny font, overlap buttons or clip the header.

- [ ] **Translation browser overlay:** Chrome translate bar is browser-controlled; verify `html lang="fi"`, UTF-8 and real language consistency, but do not misrepresent browser UI as a broken website or block translation for accessibility.
- [ ] **External AI feedback** is qualitative, not a release certificate. User/independent-human criticism takes priority for actual perceived font scale, readability and animation; benchmark on real devices and record physical scroll smoothness separately from synthetic CSS tests.

## 2026-11-02 independent human navigation and plain Finnish

- [ ] **Independent human first impression:** ask a non-building reviewer to evaluate the real site on their own device. Do not discount their response because an AI review rated it highly. Apply actionable UX feedback to every client.
- [ ] **Desktop main navigation:** meaningful primary menu text is generally 15–16px with adequate contrast, spacing and keyboard focus. Review at 1024/1180/1280/1440px, not only a single 1440px viewport. Never use tiny 12–13px site-navigation text to make an overfull header fit.
- [ ] **Phone/contact belongs to header composition:** phone link, contact CTA and brand use one coherent visual system and obvious grouping. A detached, unstyled phone number should not appear stranded between navigation and CTA. At tablet widths the number can move to a clearly labeled mobile menu while the main contact action remains visible.
- [ ] **Natural Finnish language:** check headings for awkward local idioms ("kotona tai pihalla" when repeated), exact service terms and semantically duplicate claims sitewide, not only the narrow regexes for AI antithesis slogans. Edit as real Finnish, with business facts intact.
- [ ] **Motion is subtle and performance-conscious:** optional one-shot section reveals using IntersectionObserver, transform and opacity, plus responsive focus/hover/click feedback. Keep native trackpad/touch wheel input. Initial viewport and all content remain visible without JavaScript, IntersectionObserver or with prefers-reduced-motion. Do not use scroll handlers that drive layout/position.
- [ ] **Photo brand contamination:** review legible manufacturer/product brands in stock photos, not just file license; a visible unrelated third-party logo can imply an endorsement. Prefer neutral photos and approved client-owned assets; crop only if truthful and rights-compatible.
- [ ] **About voice:** source business facts in third person where needed but write for the visitor, not as a clumsy outsider report such as "yrityksen omilla verkkosivuilla kerrotaan". Do not falsely impersonate the business's team or invent first-person claims.
- [ ] **Link delivery:** after every reviewed version publish and verify the internal preview link, then include it in the same response automatically. Sales still require a branded custom URL and explicit approval. See `docs/KAURIVO_PREVIEW_LINK_POLICY.md`.

- [ ] **Optional sections are conditional:** testimonials, team and FAQ appear only when the client supplies truthful, authorized content. Do not auto-create fake names, company history or service claims, and do not leave conspicuous empty slots.
- [ ] **Demo/publication modes:** demo badges, test-form notices, Kaurivo's test endpoint and fake/technical demonstration labels are retained in a clearly marked preview. A real delivery MUST remove or replace all demo-only content at build time *after* owner approval, including meta descriptions and footer; it must use the real approved domain, real contact handling, privacy disclosures and proven client CMS. Never suppress a demo-only legal notice by a hidden CSS class while still deploying a demo form.
- [ ] **Visual patterns:** no repeated purposeless italic heading fragments. Default zero; if an actual client brand warrants one, at most one deliberate focal use per page with human signoff. Eliminate remaining displaced decorative hero shapes and ensure nearby pricing links/text don't visually collide.
- [ ] **Small text:** body, descriptive captions and form instructions should normally be at least 14–15 px, or 13 px for secondary metadata with verified sufficient contrast. Do not use pale grey 10–11 px content copy on phones. Check actual WCAG contrast, not just hex color intent.
- [ ] **Optional location/telephone form fields:** only when the business has confirmed a legitimate need and the backend, consent and privacy storage/handling support them; do not add new personal-data collection to make a demo look more complete.
- [ ] **Pricing suggestions are not verified commercial terms:** model-generated €1,800–€3,000 / €20–€50 estimates are ideas, not Kaurivo prices. Present an actual quote only when scope, costs, VAT and owner approval are established.

## External desktop V8 review: interaction, layout and link trust — 2026-11-02

- [ ] **Natural input handling:** desktop trackpad, wheel, touch and keyboard scroll with no script-driven jank, unnecessary global smooth scrolling, hover-only transformations or paint-heavy sticky blur. Test actual physical device when possible and record measured evidence; do not claim an optimization guarantees 60 fps.
- [ ] **Real editorial hierarchy:** avoid chains of wide pale-grey bands, cramped sticky pricing sidebars, badge-like text panels, visually touching links and copy in narrow columns. Price notes, verified tax guidance and action links must have genuine breathing room on desktop and 320px mobile.
- [ ] **First-open trust is a blocker:** never link a prospect to raw.githack/rawgit.hack warning pages or any developer URL (`github.io`, `pages.dev`, `vercel.app` etc). Client-facing preview requires verified branded HTTPS domain, direct load without extra clicks/warnings and actual human/owner review. Internal free technical preview URLs are not customer links.
- [ ] **Keep tax guidance separate from pricing claims:** present official-source eligibility conditions without promising a business is registered or that every service qualifies.
- [ ] **Do not manufacture founder story:** add named real operator, portraits, reviews, service locations, FAQs, turnaround guarantees and tax eligibility only with source/owner approval. The absence of such evidence is a content gap, not an excuse to fabricate it.
- [ ] **No PII-collecting fields for optics:** do not add phone, postcode or locality to a test/demo form merely to raise a design score without a privacy and backend plan.

## Shared V6 client-feedback guard: coherent imagery, restrained affordances, truthful forms

- Each site's imagery MUST be art-directed as one coherent collection in
  lighting, season, color grade, subject matter and composition. Mixing random
  unrelated stock photos / branded employees from unrelated businesses is a
  client-facing blocker. Owner-supplied licensed photos always take priority.
  If real client photos are unavailable, prefer **fewer**, high-quality,
  stylistically consistent illustrative images, accurately labeled; never
  imply depicted models are the client's staff.
- Assume **no decorative arrows** in every button, service card, contact
  row or CTA. Only use an arrow when it conveys a genuine navigation/interaction
  need and is selectively approved. Explicit destinations and actual click
  styling are better than repeating arrow glyphs.
- Contact UX must be task-based: visitors see services and optional prices,
  then one clear enquiry path. Do not spam identical contact CTA buttons in
  each card and content block; keep a single main CTA in each meaningful view.
- Never ship a form that claims it sends the request but opens a prefilled
  mailto composer. A form must POST to a tested backend and show confirmed
  success/failure, or be explicitly replaced with honest direct phone/email
  contact links. Draft/demo test endpoints must identify the recipient and
  privacy context, and MUST NOT silently forward leads to a client.
- Verify all form behaviors in a real browser and run a controlled synthetic
  submission to confirm server-side receipt; do not claim functional form
  delivery based only on CSS, JS tests, form validation or HTML.
- Preserve real brand identity and locked slogans without using the
  same tagline in repeated boxes/sections; preserve original client photos
  by default unless explicitly approved otherwise. For customer-owned editing,
  verify a real CMS editor independently; demos are not CMS deliveries.

## Quality of execution — BLOCKERS

- [ ] Test with the **actual browser** on 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px,
      not PDF alone: menu toggle, real page navigation, scroll, buttons,
      form, image crop, text overflow, keyboard/tab focus and reduced-motion.
- [ ] Motion provides carefully staged navigation, meaningful feedback
      and depth where beneficial. No mandatory 3D gimmicks, incessant
      motion, heavy scroll hijacking, delayed readability or script-dependent
      blank pages. Static/reduced-motion path remains usable.
- [ ] Check mobile data and first open on realistic hosting. A cold-starting
      free preview host is NOT a polished customer experience. Record
      actual load time and cold-start behavior; choose adequate production
      hosting before making performance claims.
- [ ] Test site URLs and assets in deployed environment. An unlisted/noindex
      link is publicly accessible if shared; private content requires real
      access control. Distinguish preview vs published client site.
- [ ] Accessibility: meaningful landmarks/headings, correct alt text,
      keyboard controls, readable color contrast, usable tap targets.
      Automated passing is not WCAG certification.
